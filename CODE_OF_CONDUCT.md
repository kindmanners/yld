# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make the YLD community welcoming, safe, and equitable for
everyone participating in good faith. We respect different identities,
backgrounds, viewpoints, and experiences.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Respect the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
- Engage kindly, honestly, and constructively.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept useful feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.
- Protect confidential information and personal data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
unwanted sexual attention, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
impersonation, violating confidentiality, or other conduct that makes the
community unsafe. Failing to credit sources and repeatedly posting unrelated
commercial promotion are also unacceptable.

## Reporting an issue

Report possible violations privately to the YLD maintainers at
**maxiefeseymen@gmail.com**. Include a description, relevant context, and any
supporting information. Reports will be handled as promptly and confidentially
as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may investigate available messages, logs, or other evidence and
contact involved people or witnesses. They will prioritize safety, privacy,
and confidentiality while keeping enforcement as transparent as practical.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, maintainers may use a private warning,
temporary limits on participation, temporary suspension, or a permanent ban.
Conditions may include acknowledging harm, apologizing, respecting a cooling-
off period, or meeting explicit conditions before returning. Serious incidents
may skip lower levels of the enforcement ladder.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all YLD community spaces, including the GitHub
repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, official communication
channels, and events. It also applies when someone officially represents the
project in public.
